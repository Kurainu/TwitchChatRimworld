# Changelog

## 1.1.0 - 2026-08-16

- Connects to your channel on its own when a save loads, so you don't have to
  press Test Connection each session.
- Re-pick and the chatter picker now respect the shared-names setting, so a name
  won't land on two pawns of one kind when it's off.
- Wild men use the same naming rules as other pawns.
- The "no free chatter" reason shows in the Twitch tab under the button instead
  of at the top of the screen.
- Reworded the shared-names option and a few setting descriptions.
- The startup log reads its version from the assembly.
- Removed two unused language entries.

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Names animals, raiders, and visitors after recent Twitch chatters, with a
  toggle per category.
- Shows each chatter's messages as in-game speech bubbles, with Twitch, 7TV,
  BTTV, FFZ, and Unicode emotes.
- Reads chat anonymously. No Twitch login or token.
- Twitch tab on each pawn to link, re-pick, or unlink a chatter by hand.
- Moderation: ignore common chat bots and keep a per-name blacklist.
- Adjustable bubble duration and how many recent chatters are kept.
